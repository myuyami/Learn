The Lost Signal

(Short story)

For ten long years, the deep-space observatory sat in absolute silence.

The monitors showed nothing but flat, dead lines.

The technicians had all given up and gone home, leaving behind a single, forgotten terminal.

Then, at exactly midnight, the old screen flickered to life.

A single line of code began to type itself across the glowing black dashboard:

"Is anyone still out there? We finally found the way back home."

A heavy silence filled the room, broken only by the rhythmicbeep—beep—beep of the flashing monitor.

Outside the window, the stars began to blink out, one by one.

The End

The Midnight Mirror

(Short story)

My tablet screen flickered, casting a dim glow across my dark bedroom. It was 3:14 AM.

I stood up to get a glass of water, glancing casually at the old mirror hanging near my door. In the reflection, I saw my room clearly.

But my reflection didn't look like me at all, refusing to mimic my movements the way a normal mirror should.

It was sitting on my bed, staring intensely at the book i've read before that was placed on the nightstand.

Frozen in terror, I watched as the reflection slowly turned its head toward me.

A satisfied, cunning smile spread across its face, though its eyes remained hidden in the deep shadows of the room Slow and deliberate,it placed its hand flat against the book and whispered:

"Thanks for letting me out."

Then, with a sharp, heavy click, the bedroom door locked itself from the inside.

The End
