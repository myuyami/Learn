The Lost Signal

(short story)

For ten long years, the deep-space observatory sat in absolute silence.


The monitors showed nothing but flat, dead lines.
The technicians had all given up and gone home,
Leaving behind a single, forgotten terminal
Then, at exactly midnight, the old screen flickered to life.
A single line of code began to type itself across the glowing black dashboard: "Is anyone still out there? We finally found the way back home."

Outside the window, the stars began to blink out, one by one.

The End


The Midnight Mirror

My tablet screen flickered, casting a dim glow across my dark bedroom. It was 3:14 AM.


I stood up to get a glass of water, glancing casually at the old mirror hanging near my door.


In the reflection, I saw my room clearly. But my reflection wasn't looking back at me.


It was sitting on my bed, staring intensely at the book i've read before that was placed on the nightstand.

Frozen in terror, I watched as the reflection slowly turned its head toward me.


It smiled a wide, unnatural grin, tapped its finger against the glass, and whispered:


"Thanks for letting me out." Then, the bedroom door locked itself from the inside.
